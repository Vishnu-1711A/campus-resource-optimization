System Database Design & Constraints Summary

This implementation strictly enforces the relational design documented in the PES University DBMS Specification:

  • Relational Normalization: Engineered to Third Normal Form (3NF) eliminating redundancy across locations, categories, and master tables.
  • Constraint Integrity: Automatic collision prevention on overlapping datetime reservations via triggers and CHECK constraints.
  • Lifecycle Automation: Automatic status synchronization between maintenance tickets and asset availability flags.
  • Audit Ledger: Centralized booking_audit table capturing historical state transitions and cancellation reasons.
